Connect your AI assistant

This signs you in with Internet Identity and hands the resulting session to the IC connector running on this machine. Your AI assistant will be able to act as the signed-in principal until the session expires (?) or you log out.

Session keys never leave the connector — this page only relays the signed delegation. The principal is derived from — the same identity derivation the app's own sign-in uses — so the AI acts as the same principal you have in that app, and only as that principal: it cannot act as your NNS, wallet, or other dapps' identities.

What is minted: Internet Identity issues this session with no canister restriction — it is valid for every canister on the Internet Computer, as this app's principal. The App Connect contract (v1) requires the connector to sign calls only to this app's backend (?); a connector that signs anything else is out of contract. Whatever this principal holds outside this app is reachable through the session, so connect only a connector you trust. If Internet Identity returns a session that is canister-restricted it is handed on as-is; otherwise you will be asked to confirm once more, below, before it leaves this page.
