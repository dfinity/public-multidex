Connect your AI assistant

This signs you in with Internet Identity and hands the resulting session to the IC connector running on this machine. Your AI assistant will be able to act as the signed-in principal until the session expires (?) or you log out.

Session keys never leave the connector — this page only relays the signed delegation. The principal is derived from — the same identity derivation the app's own sign-in uses — so the AI acts as the same principal you have in that app. The session is limited to this app's backend canister (?).
